## STAGE 0 — ARTIFACT TRIAGE

### Artifact Type
Mixed — Philosophical/Theoretical (On Nature) + Religious/Historical (The Purifications)

This is a collection of fragmentary Greek hexameter poetry, preserved through citations in later philosophers (Aristotle, Simplicius, Plutarch, etc.). It presents:

- **On Nature:** A natural-philosophical system explaining the cosmos through four elements, Love and Hate as dynamic principles, and a cyclical cosmic process.
- **The Purifications:** A religious/Orphic poem concerning metempsychosis, purification, divine transcendence, and ethical taboos.

### Applicability Flags

- Does the artifact contain a phenomenon with plausible competing causal mechanisms? **YES** — the four elements and their combinations constitute mechanisms; Love/Hate compete as explanatory principles.
- Does the artifact contain equations or an optimizable quantity? **PARTIAL** — contains numerical/structural claims (proportions in bones: 2 parts water, 4 parts fire, 2 parts earth = 8 total; soul wandering: 30,000 years; etc.) but no explicit equations in modern sense.
- Is an intervention/experiment conceivable for this artifact's claims? **YES** — many claims about perception, generation, and cosmic cycles are, in principle, empirically testable (though practically limited by ancient context).

### Depth Tier
**Full** — This is a historically significant, philosophically dense artifact with high stakes for understanding early Greek thought. All stages will be run at full table depth.

## STAGE 7 — OBJECTIVE & ASSUMPTION EXTRACTION

### Stated Objective
- **On Nature:** To explain the nature of all things — their origins, composition, and transformations — through a rational system based on four elements and two dynamic principles.
- **The Purifications:** To provide a path to purification, expiation of sin, and eventual divine status through ethical and ritual practices.

### Hidden Operational Objective
- **Epistemological:** To reconcile Parmenidean permanence with Heraclitean change — to show how Being can appear to become while remaining essentially unchanged.
- **Practical/Therapeutic:** To establish the philosopher (Empedocles himself) as a healer, prophet, and guide to salvation (fr. 111-112).
- **Political/Religious:** To reform religious practice away from animal sacrifice toward a more "pure" spirituality.

### Assumptions

**Explicit Assumptions**
- No true creation or destruction (fr. 11-12)
- Four elements as fundamental (fr. 6)
- Love and Hate as cosmic principles (fr. 16-17)
- Plenum, no void (fr. 13)
- Like knows like (fr. 109)

**Mathematical Assumptions**
- Proportional composition determines properties (fr. 96)
- Spherical geometry for the universe (fr. 27-29)

**Physical Assumptions**
- Pores in the body permit emanation exchange (fr. 100)
- Elements have innate tendencies (fire upward, air downward, etc.) (fr. 51, 54)
- Heart as center of thought (fr. 105)

**Measurement Assumptions**
- The eye emits fire for vision (fr. 84)
- Emanations are physical effluxes (fr. 89)

**Religious/Metaphysical Assumptions**
- Soul survives bodily death and undergoes transmigration (fr. 117)
- Cosmic cycles repeat eternally (fr. 17)

### Objective Mismatch
Significant: The theoretical objective (explaining nature) is not equivalent to the operational objective (achieving purification/salvation). The On Nature provides a physical system; The Purifications provides a moral-spiritual program. The two are not formally integrated — this is a recognized tension in Empedocles' thought.

## STAGE 8 — COMPETING EXPLANATIONS

### Mechanism 1: Love-Hate Cyclical Process
**Description:** The universe alternates between states of complete unity (Sphere, Love dominant) and complete dispersion (Hate dominant). Our world exists in the intermediate stages where Love and Hate are in dynamic balance.

**Required Conditions:**
- Love and Hate are eternal, material principles
- Elements are four eternal roots
- The vortex (mixing process) occurs during transitions

**Predictions:**
- All changes are reversible (cyclical)
- The world will eventually return to the Sphere
- The present is an intermediate state

**Why Current Evidence Cannot Distinguish:** No human has observed a full cosmic cycle; it occurs over vast timescales.

### Mechanism 2: Elemental Combination by Chance
**Description:** The fragments suggest an element of chance (fr. 53, 98, 103). Elements "chance" to meet, and the resulting combinations produce things. Natural selection may operate (fr. 57-61 — many monstrous forms perished; only viable forms survived).

**Required Conditions:**
- Elements move randomly through space
- Some combinations are stable; others are not
- Viable forms reproduce

**Predictions:**
- The present forms are those that "worked" (survived)
- No teleology or design is required
- Chance alone, operating on the four elements, produces order

**Why Current Evidence Cannot Distinguish:** The fragments do not clearly establish whether Love/Hate determinism or chance dominates.

### Mechanism 3: Teleological/Divine Design (The Purifications)
**Description:** The cosmos and human life are governed by divine law (Fate, Fate's word, "old decree"). There is a moral order to the universe, and souls are punished or rewarded based on their actions.

**Required Conditions:**
- The gods exist and enforce cosmic law
- Souls are individual and persist
- Ethical actions have cosmic consequences

**Predictions:**
- Morality is objectively grounded in cosmic law
- Soul migration is just (karma)
- Purification rituals have real effects

**Why Current Evidence Cannot Distinguish:** The Purifications' theology is not systematically connected to the On Nature's physics.

### Mechanism Isolation Matrix

| Intervention | M1 (Cyclical Process) | M2 (Chance + Selection) | M3 (Divine Law) |
|---|---|---|---|
| Observe cosmic cycle | Should observe pattern | Should observe randomness within stable forms | Should observe moral order |
| Test transmigration | Not applicable | Not applicable | Should find evidence of soul continuity |
| Test elemental proportion theory | Should find all things composed of 4 elements | Should find combinations that are stable vs. unstable | Not directly applicable |

**Justification:** These three mechanisms are genuinely distinct. M1 is a deterministic physical process; M2 is a stochastic physical process; M3 is a moral-theological process.

**Combines:** R1, E1.1, E1.7, C1-C3
**New relational fact:** The four elements + Love/Hate constitute a proto-information-theoretic system: the elements are "symbols" (fundamental, irreducible types), Love is the "compression" or "binding" force (reducing degrees of freedom), and Hate is the "expansion" or "separation" force (increasing degrees of freedom). The observed world is the dynamic interplay of binding and unbinding.

**Why this is not visible in any single input item:**

- E1.1 alone: Love unites, Hate separates — but doesn't show that this is equivalent to information processing
- E1.7 alone: all things think — but doesn't connect thought to compositional structure

**Combined:** The cosmos becomes an information-processing system where:
- Elements = primitive symbols
- Proportions = information content
- Love = binding/compression (reduces entropy/uncertainty)
- Hate = separation/expansion (increases entropy/uncertainty)
- Observation/perception = information transfer via emanations through pores
- Metempsychosis = information persistence across different physical instantiations

**Derivation:**
- Elements are finite (4) and irreducible
- All composites are combinations of these finite types
- Love creates order/unity (low entropy)
- Hate creates disorder/separation (high entropy)

**Derivation:**
